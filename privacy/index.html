Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Android app HOTIANA (net.hotiana.companion), published by 16834981 Canada Inc. (“we”, “us”). HOTIANA is a personal assistant for your phone. It works as an ordinary app, can optionally act as your home screen, keeps a private vault on your phone, and can connect the AI you already use (for example Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT) so that AI can carry out actions on your phone.

HOTIANA Vantage, a separate edition with an optional accessibility service, is not yet available on Google Play. We will add it to this policy before it becomes available.

The short version

  • Your vault stays on your phone. It is not synced to any cloud. HOTIANA turns off Android backup and device-to-device transfer for all of its data.
  • Some things do leave your phone, only when you use a feature that needs it: signing in, connecting an outside AI to your phone (the bridge), cloud voice, web answers, place search, weather, reporting an AI reply, your own computer, Home media and news, and services you connect yourself. Each is described below with who receives it.
  • Nothing from your vault is shared with an outside AI unless you approve that specific answer with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.
  • You decide which outside AI can act on your phone, on which devices and with which permissions. You can see and revoke each one in Settings › Connections › Assistants with access.
  • We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or ad tracking.
  • You can delete your account in the app or by emailing privacy@hotiana.ca from your account's email address.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

1. Signing in (HOTIANA account)

When you sign in with Google, the app sends HOTIANA's identity service a one-time Google sign-in token, a random installation ID and the app type. The service returns your account ID, name, email address and profile picture link, plus session tokens. When you enroll your phone for the bridge, the app also sends a device public key, a device name and the app version. This traffic uses HTTPS (TLS); the app refuses to talk to the identity service over anything else.

Recipients: HOTIANA's identity service, operated by us on Google Cloud in Toronto, Canada (Cloud Run and a Firestore database in northamerica-northeast2), and Google for Google sign-in.

Google Cloud logs web requests to our services: the time, address requested, status, IP address and app identifier. Cloud Logging keeps these logs for 30 days. To limit repeated requests, the service also counts requests under keyed one-way codes of an IP address, installation ID, session or account. Counting windows range from 10 seconds to 24 hours; counters are kept for at most 24 hours.

2. The bridge (connecting an outside AI to your phone)

If you connect an AI such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT, that AI can ask your phone to perform actions from HOTIANA's published action list, for example reading the battery level, placing a call you confirm, or opening a screen. While HOTIANA is running, your phone keeps an outbound connection to HOTIANA's relay, which passes each request to the phone and each result back to the AI. In the Google Play edition it reconnects when the phone wakes or unlocks; Android may stop it in the background.

Before you allow access, a consent page shows the AI's name, your account, which phone and TVs it may use, and its permissions: connection and catalog (always included), open screens, run device actions, and share style dials.

  • What passes through the relay: your account and device identifiers, authorization tokens, the request, and its result (for example a battery percentage, whether a call was started, or the name and number of the person on an active call if the AI asked for call status).
  • Relay location: Google Cloud Run, Toronto, Canada (northamerica-northeast2). This connection uses TLS.
  • What the relay keeps: requests and results are not stored. A receipt of each finished action is held in memory only while your phone stays connected, for at most 24 hours.
  • What the relay logs: request metadata only: time, event, request ID, a one-way code of the phone or TV ID, status, duration and action name. These logs contain no request content or results. Google Cloud also keeps the web-request logs described above. Logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Seeing and revoking access: open Settings › Connections › Assistants with access. It lists each AI, its devices and permissions, when you approved it and when it last renewed access. Tap Revoke, then confirm. A revoked AI's next action request is refused; device listings stop within a few seconds.
  • The connected AI provider receives the results and handles them under its own privacy policy. Revoking access stops future requests; it does not erase what that provider already holds. Use the provider's own controls for that.

3. Releasing vault information to an AI

When a connected AI needs something from your vault, HOTIANA shows you the exact answer it would share. It is released only if you accept it and confirm with fresh device authentication (fingerprint, face or PIN). One approval releases one answer. If you decline or authentication fails, the answer stays on your phone. Once released, that answer is held by the AI provider under its policy.

4. Cloud voice (optional)

If you choose cloud voice, your voice audio and conversation text go directly from your phone to ElevenLabs for speech and reply generation. HOTIANA's identity service only issues the short-lived credential for that session. The microphone is used only while a voice session is active, and Android shows its microphone indicator. You can also use an ElevenLabs key of your own, stored encrypted on the phone. ElevenLabs handles the data under its own policy.

When you ask cloud voice about notifications, the information it reads is sent to ElevenLabs. Asking what an app sent today returns at most 5 notifications, each shortened to 140 characters with numbers, addresses and links removed. Reading one notification returns its title and text, up to 400 characters. Message text is read only from apps you chose; a summary of what is waiting can name apps and senders beyond those chosen apps.

Private-information access is unlocked with your fingerprint, face or device PIN for the period you choose: 1 hour by default, or 15 minutes, 4 hours, 12 hours, or until the phone locks. Vault answers still need their own approval as described above.

5. Services you connect yourself (MCP connections)

You can connect HOTIANA to other online services. Before you sign in, HOTIANA shows which server it will connect to and what that server asks for. Data goes only to that server, for the calls you choose. When you disconnect, HOTIANA revokes the token and deletes it from your phone.

6. Web answers, places and weather

  • Web answers: when you are signed in and ask a general-knowledge question that needs the web, the question text (not your name or email) goes through HOTIANA's identity service to OpenAI's API with web search. We ask OpenAI not to store these requests. Questions containing an email address, web address, coordinates, phone number, street address or postal code are refused before anything is sent.
  • Mapbox: when you ask HOTIANA to find a place, for example a shop for a pickup errand, your search words, approximate location and language are sent to Mapbox to return results (Mapbox privacy policy). Map screens are hidden in the Google Play edition.
  • Local weather: while Home is showing, your position, rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km), is sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the weather. No account or device identifier is sent. Updates occur at most once every 10 minutes after success, sooner after failure or if you tap to retry. “Local weather” is on by default and needs location permission; you can turn it off in Settings.

7. Google Play services

HOTIANA uses Google Play services for Google sign-in. The Play edition does not start Firebase Cloud Messaging, does not include the Google Home APIs, and does not send crash reports. It does not request SMS or Call Log permissions, background location or the advertising ID permission.

8. Your own computer (optional)

If you pair HOTIANA with your own computer, screen, audio, input, notifications you choose to show there, and files or text you choose to send travel directly between your phone and that computer over your Tailscale network, which encrypts them. They do not pass through HOTIANA's servers. Our identity service lists your linked computers and their network names. Every phone request is signed with a key kept in Android Keystore.

  • Desk: while a session is open, the computer's screen and sound come to the phone, and touches and typing go to the computer. Sync text clipboard is off by default and copies text both ways while focused. Use as PC microphone sends phone microphone audio to the computer when you choose it.
  • Sign-in codes: sending a code requires a fresh fingerprint or face check. The code expires on the computer within 30 seconds.
  • Call and message alerts: Share call and message alerts sends only the kind of alert, random IDs, times and sequence, with a 60-second expiry. No app name, caller, title or message text is sent this way.
  • Show my notifications on this PC: for apps you choose, the app label, title (which may name the sender) and up to 600 characters of notification text are sent. Code-like, secret and call notifications are excluded. The computer keeps at most 60 per phone, in memory only, for up to 6 hours.
  • Reply and dismiss from the PC: a separate switch lets the computer use the original app's notification actions, including while the phone is locked.
  • Send text to PC: tapping the button reads the clipboard once and sends up to 3,072 bytes. Sensitive or code-like text is refused. The computer holds it for 2 minutes and never writes its clipboard automatically.
  • Share › Send to PC: up to 20 files per send are saved in HOTIANA Transfers on your computer and stay until you delete them.
  • Receive files and text: nothing is copied or saved until you tap it. Received files are checked with SHA-256 and saved to Download/Hotiana on the phone.

9. Reporting an AI reply

If you report an AI reply and confirm sending it after a preview and a device check (fingerprint, face or PIN), HOTIANA sends our identity service the reply text (up to 20,000 characters), your chosen reason (offensive, harmful, wrong or other) and the app version. No account, name or email is attached. We keep reports for 30 days to review them, then they are deleted automatically. Check the preview because the reply text itself may contain personal information.

You can also keep a report on your phone without sending it, up to 50 per account while signed in. Account deletion cannot identify reports sent to us because they carry no account.

10. Home media and news

Home includes a YouTube panel, YouTube video and Google News widgets, and headlines from DW, Al Jazeera and Bloomberg. Loading this content contacts the relevant provider, which handles the request under its own policy.

About encryption: we state TLS only for the identity service and the bridge relay, and Tailscale encryption for your own computer. We do not claim that every connection in every feature is encrypted.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Account › Delete my HOTIANA account and data. After a fresh sign-in confirmation, we revoke every connected AI and enrolled device, remove your name, email and picture link at once, and clear HOTIANA's data on this phone once the server confirms. Remaining account records are purged within 7 days and leave backups within 30 days after that. We keep only the one-way installation code described above for 2 years. Signing in again starts a new, empty account.
  • Without the app: email privacy@hotiana.ca from the email address on your HOTIANA account and ask us to delete it. We confirm the request and delete the account and its data the same way within 30 days; the data then leaves backups within 30 days after that. See the account deletion page for both routes and their details.
  • On your phone only: you can ask HOTIANA to forget individual facts, remove documents from the documents shelf, and forget people added from contacts. To erase everything local without deleting your account, use Android Settings › Apps › HOTIANA › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall. The vault is excluded from backups, so this removes it permanently.
  • Signing out or disconnecting stops future use but does not by itself delete server records. You can revoke one AI in Settings › Connections › Assistants with access.
  • Data released to an outside AI or other provider, or sent to your own computer, must be deleted there.

No sync, no sale, no ads

HOTIANA does not sync your vault or conversations to any cloud. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising or ad tracking.

Children

HOTIANA is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change what the app collects or shares, for example when a hidden feature is turned on, we will update this policy and its date before the change ships, and ask for consent in the app where required.
