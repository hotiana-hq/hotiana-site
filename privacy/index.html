The short version

  • Your vault stays on your phone. It is not synced to any cloud and is excluded from Android backups and device transfers.
  • HOTIANA works without an account. Signing in with Google is optional. It is needed to connect an outside AI to your phone, to link your computer, and for features we run for signed-in testers (cloud voice and web answers).
  • Some things do leave your phone, only when you use a feature that needs it: signing in, connecting an AI (the “bridge”), cloud voice, web answers, Wikipedia look-ups, services you connect yourself, your own computer, and reports you choose to send. Each is described below with who receives it.
  • Nothing from your vault is shared with an outside AI unless you approve that specific answer with a fresh fingerprint or face check (or your device PIN where biometrics are not available, and in Vantage).
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads and do not use your data for advertising. We add no advertising or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account in the app or by email, and you can erase phone data at any time.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

1. Signing in (HOTIANA account, optional)

When you sign in with Google, the app sends HOTIANA's identity service a one-time Google sign-in token, a random installation ID and the app type. The service returns your account ID, name, email address and profile picture link, plus session tokens. When you link your phone to the bridge, the app also sends a device public key, a device name and the app version. This traffic uses HTTPS (TLS); the app refuses to talk to the identity service over anything else.

During the beta, sign-in is limited to invited testers. If your Google account is not on the list, the service refuses the sign-in and does not create an account. Everything else in HOTIANA keeps working without one.

Recipients: HOTIANA's identity service, operated by us on Google Cloud Run in Toronto, Canada (northamerica-northeast2), with account records in Google Cloud Firestore [OWNER: confirm the Firestore location]; and Google, for Google sign-in.

2. The bridge (connecting an outside AI to your phone)

If you connect an AI such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT, that AI can ask your phone to perform actions from HOTIANA's published action list, for example reading the battery level, placing a call you confirm, or opening a screen. Your phone keeps an outbound connection to HOTIANA's phone relay, which passes each request to the phone and each result back to the AI. In the Google Play edition this connection runs while HOTIANA is open; direct-install test builds can keep it running in the background.

  • What passes through the relay: your account and device identifiers, authorization tokens, the request, and its result (for example a battery percentage, whether a call was started, or the name and number of the person on an active call if the AI asked for call status).
  • Relay location: Google Cloud Run, Toronto, Canada (northamerica-northeast2). This connection uses TLS.
  • What the relay keeps: a receipt of each finished request is held in the relay's memory for up to 24 hours, so that a repeated request is answered without running the action twice. Receipts are not written to storage. [OWNER: confirm]
  • What the relay logs: the event, the time, an operation ID, a shortened one-way code of the device ID (never the ID itself), the status, the duration and the action name. Requests, results, arguments and tokens are never logged.
  • The AI provider you connected receives the results and handles them under its own privacy policy. Disconnecting stops future requests; it does not erase what that provider already holds. Use the provider's own controls for that.

3. Releasing vault information to an AI

When a connected AI needs something from your vault, HOTIANA shows you the exact answer it would share. It is released only if you accept it and confirm with a fresh fingerprint or face check (your device PIN is accepted where biometrics are not available, and in Vantage). One approval releases one answer, to the AI that asked. If you decline, cancel or authentication fails, the answer stays on your phone. Once released, that answer is held by the AI provider under its policy.

4. Handing a request to an AI app on your phone

You can send a request, and context you select, to an AI app such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT through Android's share sheet. Context is only included after a fresh authentication that names the app it goes to. You send it yourself in that app; HOTIANA does not send it or read the reply automatically. From that point the AI app handles it under its own policy.

5. Cloud voice (optional)

If you choose a cloud voice mode, your voice audio and conversation text go directly from your phone to the provider you selected, ElevenLabs or OpenAI, for speech and reply generation. For signed-in testers, HOTIANA's identity service issues a short-lived session credential for ElevenLabs; the conversation itself goes straight to the provider, not through our servers. You can also use a provider key of your own. The microphone is used only while a voice session is active, and Android shows its microphone indicator. These providers handle the data under their own policies. [OWNER: confirm which voice providers are offered at launch]

6. Web answers (signed-in testers)

When a signed-in tester asks a question that needs the web (a search, today's news, a translation or a definition), the question goes through HOTIANA's identity service to OpenAI, which searches the web and returns a short answer. The service refuses questions that contain an email address, a phone number, a street address or map coordinates, and asks OpenAI not to store the request. Your account identity is not sent to OpenAI.

7. Wikipedia

General-knowledge questions may send the search words (not your identity) directly to Wikipedia.

8. Services you connect yourself

You can connect HOTIANA to other online services (MCP connections). Before you sign in to one, HOTIANA shows which server it will connect to and what that server asks for. Data goes only to that server, for the calls you choose.

If you are signed in and add a connection to your account, HOTIANA saves the connection's settings to your HOTIANA account so your other devices can use it: the connection's ID, name and web address, its privacy tier and refresh schedule, whether it needs sign-in, the tools and items you selected, how they appear on Home, and when they were changed. Access and refresh tokens are never part of this. Connections you keep on one device only are not saved to your account. [OWNER: confirm the account connection-settings route is live on the identity service]

When you disconnect, HOTIANA asks the service to revoke its token, then deletes the token and the settings from your phone. Revocation depends on that service; it does not erase what the service already holds.

9. Your own computer (optional)

If you link HOTIANA with the HOTIANA Companion app on your own Windows computer, your screen, audio, input, clipboard text and files you choose travel between your phone and that computer. The link is set up through your HOTIANA account and runs over a private Tailscale network that you install on both devices; Tailscale encrypts it. [OWNER: confirm no other connection route is offered] The Windows companion is a preview and is not yet generally available.

  • Google Play edition: map, place search and navigation screens are hidden. When you ask for directions, HOTIANA hands the destination to your default maps app, which handles it under its own policy. [OWNER: confirm no Play feature sends location or search text to Mapbox]
  • Direct-install test builds: when you use HOTIANA's map, place search or navigation, your search text and location are sent to Mapbox to return results, map tiles and routes (Mapbox privacy policy). Mapbox's software can also send diagnostic and usage information about the map to Mapbox.

11. Google Play services and Firebase

  • Google sign-in uses Google Play services. Google processes the sign-in under its own policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging is included in the app. In the Google Play edition it is not started and no push token is issued. In direct-install test builds it can be used to wake HOTIANA for HOTIANA-to-HOTIANA calls and messages; Google then holds a registration token for your installation. [OWNER: confirm]

12. Reports you choose to send

  • “Report this reply” (both editions) saves an AI answer you flag, with the reason you picked, on your phone. You can export a saved report to an app you choose. If developer sending is turned on in your build, you can also send a report to us: you see exactly what will be sent, agree, and confirm with a fresh authentication for each report. Only the answer text, the reason and the app version are sent, over HTTPS. The answer text itself may contain personal information, so check it before you send. [OWNER: confirm the intake address, who receives reports and whether the intake records the signed-in account]
  • Automatic crash reports (direct-install test builds only, off until you turn them on): the report contains a report ID, the app version, the Android version, the phone model and brand, the error's stack trace with up to 20 recent in-app steps, the crash time and the app start time. It is sent to the developer's own computer over a private Tailscale network. The Google Play edition has no automatic crash reporting of ours.
  • “Report a problem” (direct-install test builds only): you write the report, can attach screenshots or a recording of HOTIANA's own window, and choose whether to include diagnostics (a filtered app log and device and performance state). It is sent to the developer's own computer only when you send it.

About encryption: we state TLS for the identity service, the phone relay and report sending, and Tailscale encryption for the computer link and crash reports. We do not claim that every connection in every feature is encrypted.

Messages and call history (SMS and Call Log)

  • Google Play edition (HOTIANA and HOTIANA Vantage): HOTIANA does not request or use SMS, MMS or Call Log permissions, and cannot read your text messages or call history. When you ask it to call or text someone, it opens your default phone or messaging app with the number or message filled in, and you finish there. If you grant notification access, HOTIANA can show message notifications and send a reply you approve through the messaging app's own reply action.
  • Direct-install test builds: HOTIANA asks for SMS and Call Log access only when you choose to make it your default SMS app or default phone app. With that access it reads your messages (sender, text, time, attachments) and recent calls (number, date, duration, type) so it can show them, let you reply or call back, and send messages you confirm. This information stays on your phone. It is only shared with a connected AI if you ask for something that needs it and the result is returned through the bridge described above. You can remove the permissions or pick a different default app at any time in Android settings.

Other permissions

Android asks you before HOTIANA can use any of these. Microphone (voice and the wake word), location (answers that need where you are), contacts and calendar (finding people and events you ask about), notification access (showing and replying to notifications), exact alarms (reminders you set) and phone (calls you start) are all optional; the app keeps working without them. HOTIANA has no background-location permission. You can revoke any of them in Android settings.

HOTIANA Vantage: the accessibility service

Vantage includes an optional Android accessibility service called “Vantage assistive notifications”. It places a thin strip along the top edge of the screen. Pulling down from that strip opens larger notification controls, and a button can open Android's own notification shade.

  • What it reads: only the position and movement of touches that start in that strip or inside the expanded controls.
  • What it does not do: it does not read screen text, does not receive accessibility events, does not inspect other apps and does not perform gestures or taps for you.
  • Notification content shown in the controls comes from the separate notification-access permission, not from the accessibility service.
  • Consent first: on first use Vantage shows a disclosure and asks you to choose “I understand and continue” or “Continue without assistive access”. Refusing keeps the strip off and the app usable. Even after you consent, the strip stays off until you also grant access in Android settings and switch it on. You can turn the strip off or revoke access in Android settings at any time.
  • Nothing read by these assistive features leaves your phone unless you take an explicit action, such as tapping Send on a reply.

The regular HOTIANA app does not include this accessibility service.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Settings, then Account, then Delete my HOTIANA account and data. Step-by-step instructions, and exactly what is deleted and when, are on the account deletion page.
  • By email: write to [OWNER: monitored privacy contact email] from the email address of your HOTIANA account. See the account deletion page.
  • Without an account: nothing about you is held on our servers. To erase everything on the phone at once, use Android Settings › Apps › HOTIANA › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Because the vault is excluded from backups, this removes it permanently. You can also ask HOTIANA to forget individual facts, remove documents and forget people added from your contacts.
  • Signing out, disconnecting or uninstalling stops future use but does not delete your account.
  • Data you released to an outside AI, a voice provider, a service you connected or your own computer must be deleted there.
