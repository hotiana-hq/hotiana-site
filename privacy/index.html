The short version

  • Your vault stays on your phone. It is not synced to any cloud. HOTIANA turns off Android backup and device-to-device transfer for all of its data.
  • HOTIANA works without an account. Signing in with Google is optional. It is needed to let an outside AI act on your phone, to link your computer, and for features we run for signed-in testers (cloud voice and web answers).
  • Some things do leave your phone, only when you use a feature that needs it: signing in, letting an outside AI act on your phone, cloud voice, web answers, local weather, YouTube and news panels on Home, services you connect yourself, your own computer, HOTIANA Care requests, and reports you choose to send (and, in direct-install test builds, maps and Google Home). Each is described below with who receives it. The list of recipients names every company involved.
  • You decide which outside AI can act on your phone, on which devices and with which permissions. You can see and revoke each one in Settings › Connections › Assistants with access.
  • Our servers keep standard request logs. When your phone contacts HOTIANA's identity service or phone relay, the request log records your IP address and app type. These logs are kept for 30 days.
  • Nothing from your vault is shared with an outside AI unless you approve that specific answer with a fresh fingerprint or face check (or your device PIN where biometrics are not available).
  • Cloud voice is different from on-device voice. If you choose a cloud voice provider, it receives your conversation and the results of what HOTIANA looks up for you, which can include your notifications, calendar and contacts while your private information is unlocked. See Cloud voice.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads of our own and do not use your data for advertising. We add no advertising or analytics tools. (Videos from YouTube can include YouTube's own ads.)
  • You can delete your account in the app or by email, and you can erase phone data at any time.

What leaves your phone, and who receives it

1. Signing in (HOTIANA account, optional)

When you sign in with Google, the app sends HOTIANA's identity service a one-time Google sign-in token, a random installation ID and the app type. The service returns your account ID, name, email address and profile picture link, plus session tokens. The app may load your profile picture from that link (normally a Google address) to show it. When you link your phone to the bridge, the app also sends a device public key, a device name and the app version. This traffic uses HTTPS (TLS); the app refuses to talk to the identity service over anything else.

During the beta, sign-in is limited to invited testers. If your Google account is not on the list, the service refuses the sign-in and does not create an account. The attempt still appears in the request log described below. Everything else in HOTIANA keeps working without an account.

What the identity service logs. Like every Google Cloud Run service, it keeps a request log of every request it receives, including a refused sign-in. Each entry records:

  • your IP address;
  • the user agent (the app and Android version);
  • the address requested;
  • the time, the response status and how long the request took.

These logs are kept in Google Cloud Logging for 30 days and are used to run and protect the service [OWNER: confirm, or add a Cloud Logging exclusion and change this text]. The service's own error log records only the error type, where in the code it happened, the route and a request ID. To limit repeated requests (for example sign-in attempts, web answers and voice sessions), the service also counts requests under a keyed one-way code made from your IP address, installation ID, session or account. Each count is deleted when its time window ends, which is between 10 seconds and 24 hours [OWNER: confirm the Firestore TTL policy on rate windows].

Recipients: HOTIANA's identity service, operated by us on Google Cloud Run in Toronto, Canada (northamerica-northeast2), with account records in Google Cloud Firestore [OWNER: confirm the Firestore location]; and Google, for Google sign-in.

2. Letting an outside AI act on your phone (the bridge)

You can let an AI such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT act on your phone, and on any HOTIANA TV linked to your account [OWNER: HOTIANA TV is not on Google Play; confirm how to describe TVs at launch]. To connect, the AI sends you to HOTIANA's sign-in page. After you sign in, a consent page shows the AI's name, your account, and which phone and TVs it may use, and lets you choose its permissions:

  • Connection and catalog (always included): see that your devices are connected and which actions they offer;
  • Open screens: open HOTIANA screens on your phone;
  • Run device actions: run the actions each device allows, with the same confirmations as on the phone (on a TV, only while control is allowed on that TV);
  • Share style dials: read your visual style settings.

The AI can then ask your phone to perform actions from HOTIANA's published action list, for example reading the battery level, placing a call you confirm, or opening a screen. Your phone keeps an outbound connection to HOTIANA's phone relay, which passes each request to the phone and each result back to the AI. In the Google Play edition this connection runs while HOTIANA is running and reconnects when the phone wakes or unlocks; Android may stop it in the background. Direct-install test builds can keep it running in the background and after a restart, with a notification.

  • What passes through the relay: your account and device identifiers, authorization tokens, the request, and its result (for example a battery percentage, whether a call was started, or the name and number of the person on an active call if the AI asked for call status).
  • Relay location: Google Cloud Run, Toronto, Canada (northamerica-northeast2). This connection uses TLS.
  • What the relay keeps: a receipt of each finished action is held in the relay's memory while your phone stays connected, so that a repeated request is answered without running the action twice. Receipts older than 24 hours are removed. Receipts are not written to storage. [OWNER: confirm]
  • What the relay logs: the relay's own log records the event, the time, an operation ID, a shortened one-way code of the phone or TV ID (never the ID itself), the status, the duration and the action name. Requests, results, arguments and tokens are never logged there. Separately, Google Cloud Run keeps a request log for each connection to the relay: your IP address, the user agent, the address requested, the time, the status and how long the connection lasted. Both logs are kept in Google Cloud Logging for 30 days. [OWNER: confirm, or add a Cloud Logging exclusion and change this text]
  • What our identity service keeps about each AI you allow (an “access grant”): the AI's name and app ID, which phone and TVs it may use, the permissions you chose, a one-way code of its secret, and when it was approved, last renewed and expires.
  • Seeing and revoking access: open Settings › Connections › Assistants with access. It lists each AI with the devices it can use, its permissions in plain words, when you approved it and when it last renewed its access. Tap Revoke, then confirm. The relay then refuses that AI's next action request, and stops showing it your devices within a few seconds.
  • The AI provider you connected receives the results and handles them under its own privacy policy. Revoking access stops future requests; it does not erase what that provider already holds. Use the provider's own controls for that.

3. Releasing vault information to an AI

When a connected AI needs something from your vault, HOTIANA shows you the exact answer it would share. It is released only if you accept it and confirm with a fresh fingerprint or face check (your device PIN is accepted where biometrics are not available). One approval releases one answer, to the AI that asked. If you decline, cancel or authentication fails, the answer stays on your phone. Once released, that answer is held by the AI provider under its policy.

4. Handing a request to an AI app on your phone

You can send a request, and context you select, to an AI app such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT through Android's share sheet. Context is only included after a fresh authentication that names the app it goes to. You send it yourself in that app; HOTIANA does not send it or read the reply automatically. From that point the AI app handles it under its own policy.

5. Cloud voice (optional)

If you choose cloud voice, HOTIANA connects your phone directly to ElevenLabs, which recognises your speech, writes the assistant's replies and speaks them. During a cloud voice conversation ElevenLabs receives:

  • your voice audio and the text of the conversation;
  • the assistant's settings for the conversation (its personality and reply style);
  • when HOTIANA starts the conversation for you, what it is about. For example, in direct-install test builds that are your default phone app, HOTIANA can say who is calling;
  • while your private information is unlocked, a short record of your previous conversation with HOTIANA, so the assistant can pick up where you left off;
  • the results of what the assistant does or looks up on your phone for you. Many results are plain, such as the battery level, what is playing or the local weather. Some contain your own information, for example:
    • the app, sender, title and text of a notification you ask it to read (up to 400 characters);
    • the apps and senders of notifications that are waiting;
    • calendar event titles and times;
    • your reminders;
    • the name of the person a call or message is for.
    In direct-install test builds the results can also include your recent calls and messages, and where you are, when you ask for them.

HOTIANA only reads your own information (notifications, calendar, reminders, contacts and, in direct-install test builds, call and message history and location) while your private information is unlocked. You unlock it with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. It then stays open for the period you choose in settings: 1 hour by default, or 15 minutes, 4 hours, 12 hours, or until the phone locks. You are not asked again for each answer. If your phone has no screen lock, these reads are not locked. Facts stored in your vault are handled differently: each one still needs its own approval (section 3). With on-device voice, none of this leaves your phone. [OWNER: D-F open. For signed-in testers, HOTIANA's identity service issues a short-lived ElevenLabs session credential. The conversation itself goes straight to ElevenLabs, not through our servers. You can also use an ElevenLabs key of your own, which is stored encrypted on the phone. The app also contains an OpenAI voice option, but HOTIANA's service does not issue credentials for it, so the Google Play edition does not use it. The microphone is used only while a voice session is active, and Android shows its microphone indicator. A conversation can continue while you use another screen. ElevenLabs handles the data under its own policy. [OWNER: declare these flows (including notification content, calendar and contacts shared with the voice provider) in the Play Data safety form]

6. Web answers (signed-in testers)

When a signed-in tester asks a question that needs the web (a search, today's news, a translation or a definition), the question goes through HOTIANA's identity service to OpenAI (its Responses API, with web search), which searches the web and returns a short answer. The service refuses questions that contain an email address, a web address, a phone number, a street address, a postal code or map coordinates, and asks OpenAI not to store the request. Your account identity is not sent to OpenAI. Asking OpenAI not to store the request is not a guarantee about OpenAI's own logs, which follow OpenAI's policy.

Access and refresh tokens are never part of this. Connections you keep on one device only are not saved to your account. 8. Your own computer (optional)

If you link HOTIANA with the HOTIANA Companion app on your own Windows computer, data travels directly between your phone and that computer. It does not pass through HOTIANA's servers: our identity service only keeps the list of computers linked to your account (see the retention table) and tells your phone each computer's network name. The link runs over a private Tailscale network that you install on both devices and sign in to yourself; Tailscale encrypts the connection, and HOTIANA does not add a second layer of encryption on top. HOTIANA only connects to computer addresses on your Tailscale network, and every request from the phone is signed with a key kept in your phone's Android Keystore.

  • Desk (using your computer from the phone): your computer's screen and sound come to the phone, and your touches and typing go to the computer while a Desk session is open. If you turn on Sync text clipboard (off by default), clipboard text is copied both ways during the session. If you choose Use as PC microphone, your phone's microphone audio goes to the computer.
  • Sign-in codes: you can send a sign-in code from a notification to your computer after a fresh fingerprint or face check. It expires on the computer within 30 seconds.
  • Call and message alerts: if you turn on Share call and message alerts, your computer is told that a call, message or notification arrived from an app you chose, with times only. No app name, caller, title or message text is sent this way.
  • Phone link [OWNER: phone link is not yet merged into the release build; remove this item if it does not ship]:
    • Show my notifications on this PC (off until you turn it on): for apps you choose, the app name, title (often the sender's name) and text (up to 600 characters) of new notifications. Notifications that look like sign-in codes, are marked secret, are calls, or come from apps you did not choose are not sent. The computer keeps them in memory only, at most 60, for up to 6 hours.
    • Reply and dismiss from the PC (a separate switch): your computer can send a reply or dismiss a notification. The phone carries it out through the original app's own notification buttons, also while the phone is locked.
    • Send text to PC: when you tap it, your phone's current clipboard text (up to 3 KB) is sent once. Text marked sensitive, or that looks like a code, is refused. The computer holds it for 2 minutes and never puts it on its own clipboard by itself.
    • Share › Send to PC: files you share (up to 20 at a time) are saved in the HOTIANA Transfers folder on your computer, and stay there until you delete them.
    • Receive files and text (a switch): your computer can offer files and text to the phone. Nothing is copied or saved until you tap it; files are saved in Download/Hotiana on the phone.
  • Agents on your computer (not in the Google Play edition): if your computer runs HOTIANA's agent helper, you can send an instruction (up to 8,000 characters) from the phone to an AI agent running there, such as Claude Code or Codex, and see its latest output. The agent runs under your own sign-in with that AI's provider (for example Anthropic or OpenAI), which receives your instruction under its own policy. When an agent finishes, the phone shows a notification naming the agent. [OWNER: confirm no Play route (including voice) reaches PC agents; name the model provider behind the OpenClaw agent if it ships]

The Windows companion is a preview and is not yet generally available. What reaches your computer is stored there, not by us, and is deleted there.

Messages and call history (SMS and Call Log)

  • Google Play edition: HOTIANA does not request or use SMS, MMS or Call Log permissions, and cannot read your text messages or call history. When you ask it to call or text someone, it opens your default phone or messaging app with the number or message filled in, and you finish there. If you grant notification access, HOTIANA can show message notifications and send a reply you approve through the messaging app's own reply action. Because message notifications contain message text, that text can reach a cloud voice provider when you ask the assistant to read it (see Cloud voice).
  • Direct-install test builds: HOTIANA asks for SMS and Call Log access only when you choose to make it your default SMS app or default phone app. With that access it reads your messages (sender, text, time, attachments) and recent calls (number, date, duration, type) so it can show them, let you reply or call back, and send messages you confirm. This information stays on your phone. It is only shared with a connected AI if you ask for something that needs it and the result is returned through the bridge described above. You can remove the permissions or pick a different default app at any time in Android settings.

Other permissions

Android asks you before HOTIANA can use any of these. HOTIANA Vantage: the accessibility service

This section applies only to HOTIANA Vantage test builds. Vantage includes an optional Android accessibility service called “Vantage assistive notifications”. It places a thin strip along the top edge of the screen. Pulling down from that strip opens larger notification controls, and a button can open Android's own notification shade.

  • What it reads: only the position and movement of touches that start in that strip or inside the expanded controls.
  • What it does not do: it does not read screen text, does not receive accessibility events, does not inspect other apps and does not perform gestures or taps for you.
  • Notification content shown in the controls comes from the separate notification-access permission, not from the accessibility service.
  • Consent first: on first use Vantage shows a disclosure and asks you to choose “I understand and continue” or “Continue without assistive access”. Refusing keeps the strip off and the app usable. Even after you consent, the strip stays off until you also grant access in Android settings and switch it on. You can turn the strip off or revoke access in Android settings at any time.
  • Nothing read by these assistive features leaves your phone unless you take an explicit action, such as tapping Send on a reply.

The HOTIANA app on Google Play does not include this accessibility service.
