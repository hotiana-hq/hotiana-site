Support

For help with HOTIANA, questions about your privacy, or to report a problem, email us.

Email: uzair@hotiana.ca

Please include which device you use (Android, Windows, or an Apple device) and what happened.

Privacy and your account

Read what HOTIANA keeps on your phone, what leaves it and who receives it in the privacy policy.

To delete your HOTIANA account and data, use Settings › Account › Delete my HOTIANA account and data in the app. Our server accepts a deletion only within 10 minutes of signing in. If you signed in earlier, tap Sign out and Sign in with Google again first. You can also follow the steps on the account deletion page, which also explains what is deleted and when.

Apple devices

HOTIANA for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro and CarPlay is in development and not yet available. If you are part of a test group, use the email above for help.
