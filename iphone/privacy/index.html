HOTIANA Home for iPhone: privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app HOTIANA Home (net.hotiana.home), including its widgets, controls, Live Activities and Siri actions, published by 16834981 Canada Inc. (“we”, “us”). The Android app has its own policy.

The short version

  • We collect nothing. The iPhone app has no account, no HOTIANA server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • What you do in the app stays on your iPhone, or goes only where you send it: your own PC, Apple's own apps, or an AI provider you set up yourself.
  • Each permission is asked for only the first time you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

What the app uses, and why

  • Your PC (Desk). When you pair a PC, the app keeps its address and a key that proves it's your iPhone. The screen, what you type and the clipboard travel directly between your iPhone and your own PC over your own network.
  • Apple Home (House). The app reads and controls your accessories and scenes through Apple's HomeKit. Nothing about them leaves your iPhone. The names of your scenes are shared with the app's own widgets on the same iPhone.
  • Local network. Used only to reach your own PC.
  • Contacts. Used to find the person you ask to call, text or email. Calls, texts and emails open in Apple's own apps, where you send them yourself.
  • Location, while using the app. Used once when you ask where you are or to remember where you parked. Only the parking spot is saved, on your iPhone, and it shows in the app's widgets and Live Activity.
  • Calendar, add only. Used to add an event you ask for. The app can't read your calendar.
  • Apple Intelligence. On supported iPhones the Assistant uses Apple's on-device model, so what you say stays on your iPhone.

An AI provider you choose (optional)

You can add your own Anthropic (Claude) or OpenRouter API key in the Assistant's brain settings. The key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain, isn't included in backups, and is sent only to that provider.

Nothing is sent until you turn on “Send my Assistant questions to” that provider. After that, only the questions you type or say in the Assistant go to it, under your own account and that provider's terms and privacy policy. Commands, Apple Home, contacts, messages and location are always handled on your iPhone and are never sent. With OpenRouter, the app asks for providers that keep no data and don't train on it. You can remove the key or switch back to on-device answers at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date before the change ships.
