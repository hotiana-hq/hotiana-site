A personal assistant that keeps what it learns on your own devices.

HOTIANA helps with your day: notes, reminders, calls and messages, maps, and the work your own computer can do for you. It is designed to keep your information on your phone, and to keep working when your computer is off.

Where HOTIANA is today

In testing

Android

The HOTIANA Android app is being tested through Google Play.

In use

Windows computer

HOTIANA Companion connects your phone to your own Windows computer, so tasks can continue there.

In development

Apple devices

Apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro and CarPlay are being built. They are not available yet.

Built around your privacy

HOTIANA has no advertising, and adds no tracking or analytics tools of its own. Read exactly what leaves your device in the privacy policy.
