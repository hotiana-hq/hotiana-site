Delete your HOTIANA account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for HOTIANA and HOTIANA Vantage on Android, published by 16834981 Canada Inc. [OWNER: confirm the developer name exactly as shown on the Google Play listing]

[OWNER: do not publish this page until account deletion is switched on in the live identity service and a test account has been deleted end to end]

An account is optional: HOTIANA works without one. If you never signed in, nothing about you is held on our servers; see If you never signed in.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open HOTIANA on a phone where you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete my HOTIANA account and data and read the list of what will be deleted.
  4. If HOTIANA finds data saved on the phone from before you had an account, it lists it. Choose whether to erase that too; it is kept unless you choose to erase it.
  5. Tap Confirm account deletion and confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.

Cancel keeps your account and data intact. Once the request has been sent to our server, deletion cannot be cancelled. Your phone data is cleared only after our server confirms the account is deleted; if it cannot confirm, nothing is cleared and HOTIANA tells you.

Delete your account by email

If you no longer have the app or the phone, email [OWNER: monitored privacy contact email] from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in to HOTIANA, with the subject “Delete my HOTIANA account”. We may ask you to confirm the request. We complete it within [OWNER: e.g. 30 days] and tell you when it is done.

Deleting by email removes the account on our servers. Data stored on a phone stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app on that phone.

What is kept

  • A one-way code made from the deleted app installation's random ID, with no name, email address or account attached, for up to 2 years, so sign-in credentials from that installation cannot be reused. [OWNER: confirm]
  • Records we are required by law to keep. If that applies to you, we will tell you what and for how long.
  • Anything you already shared with an outside AI (such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT), a voice provider, a service you connected, or your own computer. Those copies are held by that service and must be deleted there.

Signing out, disconnecting an AI or uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Use one of the routes above.

If you never signed in

Everything HOTIANA keeps is on your phone. To erase all of it at once, open Android Settings › Apps › HOTIANA › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. The vault is excluded from backups, so this removes it permanently. You can also ask HOTIANA to forget individual facts, remove documents and forget people added from your contacts.
