Delete your HOTIANA account

Last updated 23 October 2026

There are two ways to delete your account and data for HOTIANA on Android, published by 16834981 Canada Inc.: in the app or by email.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open HOTIANA on a phone where you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings › Account › Delete my HOTIANA account and data and read what will be deleted.
  3. If HOTIANA lists data saved on the phone before you had an account, choose whether to erase it too. It is kept unless you choose to erase it.
  4. Tap Confirm account deletion and confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.
  5. If your sign-in is older than 10 minutes, tap Sign in with Google again on the same screen and choose the same Google account. Then tap Confirm account deletion again within ten minutes.

Cancel keeps your account and data intact. Once the request has been sent, deletion cannot be cancelled. Your phone data is cleared only after our server confirms deletion; if it cannot confirm, nothing is cleared and HOTIANA tells you.

When deletion is confirmed, access is revoked and your name, email and picture link are removed at once. Remaining account records are purged within 7 days and leave backups within 30 days after that.

2. Delete your account by email

If you no longer have the app or phone, email privacy@hotiana.ca from the email address on your HOTIANA account and ask us to delete your account and data. We will confirm the request and delete the account and its data the same way within 30 days. The data then leaves our backups within 30 days after that.

Email deletion cannot erase data stored on your phone. Clear HOTIANA's storage or uninstall it on that phone to remove local data.

What is deleted, and when

Below, “immediately” means when the deletion is processed. In-app deletion purges remaining account records within 7 days. For email requests, account deletion and the removal of its data are completed within 30 days of the request.

What is kept

  • Only a one-way code of each app installation your account used is kept for 2 years, with no name, email or account attached, so a deleted account's installation cannot be reused.
  • Data already released to an outside AI, voice provider or connected service is held under that provider's policy and must be deleted through its controls. Copies sent to your own computer must be deleted there.

Signing out, disconnecting or uninstalling does not delete your account. Use one of the two routes above.

Local data and individual AI access

To stop one AI without deleting your account, open Settings › Connections › Assistants with access, tap Revoke and confirm.

You can ask HOTIANA to forget individual facts, remove documents from the documents shelf, and forget people added from contacts. To erase all local data without deleting an account, use Android Settings › Apps › HOTIANA › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. The vault is excluded from backups, so this removes it permanently.
