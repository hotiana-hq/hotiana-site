Delete your HOTIANA account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for HOTIANA on Android, published by 16834981 Canada Inc. The same steps apply to HOTIANA Vantage test builds. [OWNER: confirm the developer name exactly as shown on the Google Play listing]

[OWNER: do not publish this page until (1) account deletion is switched on in the live identity service (it is off on the serving revision hotiana-identity-tvtick-504e51b; the prepared switch is PLAYTEST_MODE=deletion in deploy/play-test-deploy.sh), (2) the daily purge job is running (deploy/purge-job.sh), (3) a test account has been deleted end to end in the app, including the “Sign in with Google again” step, and (4) the email route below can be carried out (see the note in that section)]

An account is optional: HOTIANA works without one. If you never signed in, we hold no account or profile about you; see If you never signed in.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open HOTIANA on a phone where you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Tap Delete my HOTIANA account and data and read the list of what will be deleted.
  4. If HOTIANA finds data saved on the phone from before you had an account, it lists it. Choose whether to erase that too; it is kept unless you choose to erase it.
  5. Tap Confirm account deletion and confirm with your fingerprint, face or device PIN.
  6. If HOTIANA asks you to sign in again: for your protection, our server accepts a deletion only within 10 minutes of signing in. If your sign-in is older, HOTIANA shows “Sign in with Google again, then confirm account deletion.” and nothing is deleted. Tap Sign in with Google again on the same screen and choose the same Google account. When HOTIANA says “Signed in again. Confirm account deletion within ten minutes.”, tap Confirm account deletion again.

Cancel keeps your account and data intact. Once the request has been sent to our server, deletion cannot be cancelled. Your phone data is cleared only after our server confirms the account is deleted; if it cannot confirm, nothing is cleared and HOTIANA tells you. If HOTIANA says account deletion is unavailable, nothing was deleted: try again later, or contact us (see Questions below).

Only want to stop one AI? You do not need to delete your account. Open Settings › Connections › Assistants with access, tap Revoke next to that AI and confirm.

What is kept

  • A one-way code made from each deleted app installation's random ID, with no name, email address or account attached, for 2 years, so sign-in credentials from that installation cannot be reused.
  • Records we are required by law to keep. If that applies to you, we will tell you what and for how long.
  • Anything you already shared with an outside AI (such as Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT), a voice provider, a service you connected, HOTIANA Care or your own computer. Those copies are held by that service and must be deleted there.

Signing out, revoking an AI or uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Use one of the routes above.

If you never signed in

We hold no account or profile about you. If your phone contacted our services, for example in a refused sign-in attempt, only our request logs (with your IP address and app type) and short-lived request counters remain. They are deleted within 30 days. Everything else HOTIANA keeps is on your phone. To erase all of it at once, open Android Settings › Apps › HOTIANA › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app. HOTIANA's data is excluded from backups, so this removes it permanently. You can also ask HOTIANA to forget individual facts, remove documents and forget people added from your contacts.
